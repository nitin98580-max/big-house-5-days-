BIG HOUSE: 5 DAYS

Realistic Horror Edition

A rotting five-floor mansion & flooded basement. Four deadly residents: Granny, Grandpa, the Gardener, and the Butcher Cook.

Each day, search drawers to find 2 golden keys. Survive all 5 days to gather 10 keys, unlock the 10 heavy padlocks on the front door (hold E), open the door (E), and escape alive.

WASD Move  ·  Mouse Look  ·  Shift Run (Makes Noise!)  ·  F Toggle Torch  ·  E Open/Close Doors & Stairs, Search Drawers & Hide  ·  Stairs Walk up/down steps
